# English VO script (8 segs, edge-tts en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural + warm felt-piano BGM)

0. When an aging parent lives alone, a whole day of silence makes your heart race. Yet cameras invade their privacy, and daily check-in alarms feel like a roll call.
1. So we built PicoCare — a personal care secretary for aging parents. The phone watches quietly on-device. The secretary thinks with Meta AI. And the family finally rests easy.
2. On Ken's phone, there are no complex menus or open-ended chatbots. He can check in or request help in one tap. When something changes, PicoCare asks him first with simple options, respecting his autonomy.
3. Throughout the day, the secretary turns real phone clues into a living feed. 8:25 AM: the screen lights up for the first time. Midnight: the phone is still active. Every post cites its exact timestamp and on-device evidence — never invented.
4. And once a week, Meta's open-weight model reads seven days of findings into one warm paragraph for the family. No mystery: it states plainly that it was crafted by the open model.
5. On the caregiver app, family members see the exact same Ken. Help requests, the weekly review, and the evidence timeline are all in one place, ready to mark seen and log follow-up calls.
6. Under the hood, local rules stand guard: thin evidence means silence. Meta Muse fuses weak signals into one caring question, and triages free-text replies. Without Meta, it's just an alarm; with Meta, it's a thoughtful secretary.
7. No medical advice. No false alarms. Only care. PicoCare.
